Eligibility For Treatment.

Any individual who has tried diet and exercise but failed to lose weight is considered as a good candidate for knee treatment. Although each patient's condition is different, it is important to speak to your doctor about your options and whether surgery is right for you.

In general, patients may be a good option if:

  • There is persistent knee pain and stiffness.
  • Pain limits daily activities and quality of life.
  • Non-surgical treatment has not provided enough relief.
  • The doctor recommends surgery after evaluation.

Preparation Before Treatment.

Before treatment, your doctor will review your medical history, examine your knee and may recommend tests to ensure you are fit for surgery. Follow all instructions provided by your healthcare team.

For your safety, discuss your medicines and health conditions with your doctor before the procedure.

  • Complete the recommended medical tests.
  • Follow the doctor's instructions about medicines.
  • Arrange support for the recovery period.
  • Follow the hospital's pre-surgery instructions.

About Treatment.

Total knee replacement is a surgical procedure in which damaged surfaces of the knee joint are replaced with artificial components. The aim is to reduce pain and improve movement.

The exact procedure depends on the patient's condition and the surgeon's assessment.

  • Pre-operative assessment is completed before surgery.
  • The damaged joint surfaces are prepared carefully.
  • Artificial components are placed to restore joint function.
  • The patient is monitored after the procedure.

Post-Treatment Care Treatment.

After surgery, the healthcare team will monitor recovery and provide instructions for wound care, medicines, mobility and physiotherapy. Follow-up appointments help the doctor track progress.

Good post-treatment care supports a safe and gradual return to normal activities.

  • Take prescribed medicines as directed.
  • Keep the surgical area clean and follow wound-care instructions.
  • Attend physiotherapy and follow exercise guidance.
  • Attend all scheduled follow-up appointments.

Treatment Recovery Tips.

Recovery after knee replacement varies from person to person. Follow your doctor's advice and gradually increase activity as your strength and mobility improve.

In general, patients may benefit from:

  • Following the recommended physiotherapy plan.
  • Maintaining a healthy lifestyle during recovery.
  • Using walking support as advised by the healthcare team.
  • Reporting unusual pain, swelling or other concerns to the doctor.
